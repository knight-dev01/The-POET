A personal site that doubles as a **knowledge hub**: projects, articles and topic pages, all driven by markdown files in the repo.

## Highlights

- Markdown content with frontmatter for SEO metadata
- Topic/keyword hub with related posts
- Visual editing via Decap CMS at `/admin`
- Auto-generated sitemap, robots.txt and RSS feed
- Dark/light theme with smooth transitions
